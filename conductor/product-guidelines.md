# Product Guidelines

## Prose Style

The primary prose style for all project documentation and user-facing content will be **Technical and Precise**. This style emphasizes:

*   **Accuracy:** All information conveyed must be factually correct and verifiable.
*   **Clarity:** Content should be easily understandable, avoiding jargon where simpler terms suffice, or explaining technical terms clearly when necessary.
*   **Conciseness:** Information should be presented efficiently, without unnecessary words or elaborate phrasing.
*   **Objectivity:** While the tone can be engaging, the core information should remain objective and focused on facts.

This approach is particularly suitable for a technical project like a game, ensuring that specifications, development guides, and even in-game descriptions are unambiguous and helpful. It supports clear communication among developers and provides clear information to players without excessive fluff.

## Brand Messaging

Given the game's theme of a massively multiplayer game set in a world with dinosaurs, the brand messaging should evoke a sense of:

*   **Adventure and Exploration:** Highlight the thrill of discovering new lands and ancient creatures.
*   **Survival and Challenge:** Emphasize the harsh realities and strategic depth required to thrive in a prehistoric world.
*   **Community and Collaboration:** For the multiplayer aspect, underscore the importance of alliances, cooperation, and social interaction.

## Visual Identity

The visual identity should align with the prehistoric, survival theme, featuring:

*   **Realistic or Stylized Prehistoric Flora and Fauna:** Detailed and immersive environments and creature designs.
*   **Rugged and Natural Aesthetics:** Interface elements and in-game assets should reflect the wild, untamed nature of the setting.
*   **Dynamic and Visceral Combat:** Visuals that convey the impact and excitement of encounters with dinosaurs.

## Target Audience

The primary target audience includes:

*   Players interested in survival, exploration, and crafting games.
*   Fans of prehistoric themes and dinosaurs.
*   Individuals seeking a challenging and social multiplayer experience.
